## Which entry option fits your trip?

Start with the passport you will use and your actual itinerary. Living in Mexico is not the same as holding a Mexican passport; this guide covers Mexican ordinary passport holders.

| Your plan | Entry option | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A regular mainland China holiday without qualifying transit | Tourist visa (L) | Apply before departure |
| China between two different countries or regions | Possible 240-hour visa-free transit | Exact flight sequence, designated ports, permitted areas and onward ticket |
| A holiday entirely within Hainan | Possible 30-day Hainan visa-free entry | Enter through a Hainan open port and remain within Hainan |
| Work or study | Appropriate visa and permissions | Tourism and transit exemptions do not cover these activities |

Mexico is absent from the [official nationwide unilateral visa-free list](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183390/content.html). That does not mean every visit requires a visa: the transit and Hainan options have their own conditions. Choose the entry basis before arranging the rest of your journey.

## Can Mexicans visit China without a visa?

Yes, in specific circumstances. [Mexico is eligible for 240-hour transit](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183412/content.html). For a trip confined to Hainan, Mexican ordinary passport holders are also covered by the [30-day regional exemption](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c180637/content.html). Neither option provides unrestricted nationwide 30-day entry.

A Hainan beach holiday and a Shanghai–Dali–Lijiang holiday need different planning. Do not use the Hainan exemption as permission to continue to Yunnan or other mainland destinations. Hong Kong and Macao also have separate entry rules; a mainland visa answer should not be applied automatically to either destination.

## 240-hour visa-free transit: does your route qualify?

The basic route is **country or region A → mainland China → a different country or region B**. A Mexican passport establishes nationality eligibility, not route eligibility. Check the countries immediately before and after the mainland segment, including connections, rather than only the first and last cities of the entire holiday.

### Route examples for Mexican passport holders

These examples illustrate the route test only. They are not flight recommendations or entry approvals.

| Actual flight sequence | Route test | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mexico → Shanghai → Mexico | Does not meet the third-country test | The preceding and following country are the same |
| Mexico → Shanghai → Japan | May meet the route test | The preceding and following countries differ |
| Mexico → United States → Shanghai → United States → Mexico | Does not meet the third-country test | The mainland segment is United States → China → United States |
| Mexico → United States → Shanghai → Japan → Mexico | May meet the route test | The mainland segment is United States → China → Japan |

Other transit requirements include an ordinary passport, a confirmed onward ticket with departure date and seat arrangements, designated entry and exit ports, and a stay inside permitted areas. The official policy allows tourism during transit. Its stay clock starts at midnight following entry; follow the deadline given by immigration. See the [NIA entry requirements](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147468/c187308/content.html).

### Where can you travel during transit?

Use the current official **Eligible Ports and Permitted Stay Areas** table linked from the NIA policy. Arrival at Shanghai Pudong does not mean the permitted area is simply Shanghai Municipality. Equally, an eligible passport does not make every city or border crossing eligible.

Write down each intended stop and your exit port before choosing a domestic flight or train. If the route changes, check it again. Our [Visa Checker](/china-visa-checker#check-visa) is a planning aid; verify the permitted travel areas against the official table and your airline before relying on it.

## When do Mexican passport holders need a Chinese visa?

For an ordinary mainland holiday that does not meet an exemption, arrange a tourist visa before travel. A longer or more complex route can be easier to plan with a visa than around transit restrictions. The visa's entries, validity and permitted duration are separate items: check all three when it is issued.

For example, leaving mainland China for Hong Kong and returning later means another mainland entry. Do not assume a single-entry visa will cover both visits. Likewise, changing an onward flight can change a previously qualifying transit plan.

Use the visa category matching your purpose. A tourist itinerary is different from employment, study or reporting. See our [general China visa guide](/do-i-need-a-visa-for-china) for the wider decision process.

## How to apply for a China tourist visa in Mexico

The Chinese Embassy's [Spanish-language application requirements](https://mx.china-embassy.gov.cn/esp/LSFW/consular1/202510/t20251001_11722302.htm) direct ordinary applicants to the visa centre's online system. Register, complete the form and upload documents, then follow the centre's instructions for the remaining steps. Online submission is not visa issuance.

### Basic documents to prepare

- Passport with more than six months' validity and two consecutive blank pages, plus its data-page copy.
- Online application and a white-background colour photo.
- Tourist itinerary, return-flight reservations and hotel reservations.
- Additional documents requested for your circumstances, including legal-stay evidence when applying outside your country of nationality.

### Fees and processing time

The embassy lists regular collection in Mexico City at four working days and urgent collection at three; Tijuana collection is approximately 10–15 working days. These are published estimates, not guarantees or the total online-review timeline. It recommends applying at least a month ahead.

Check the centre's current fee schedule: visa charges and service charges are separate. Confirm appointment, passport-submission and fingerprint arrangements directly with your centre; older notices may contain expired exemptions.

## Common mistakes before booking

**Confusing a US visa with Chinese entry permission.** A US visa can matter for a US connection, but it does not grant permission to enter mainland China. Treat each country's requirements separately.

**Checking only nationality.** “Mexicans are eligible” is not enough to decide whether your flights, stay and destinations qualify for transit.

**Treating ten days as nationwide freedom.** Check the designated areas and ports, not just the duration. Keep your route and onward booking consistent with the entry basis.

**Leaving preparation until departure.** Put passport details, flight bookings, accommodation addresses and application records together early. Resolve discrepancies before airport check-in.
