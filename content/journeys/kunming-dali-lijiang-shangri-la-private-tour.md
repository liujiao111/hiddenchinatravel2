## Why this journey

This eight-day private route is a reference plan linking Yunnan’s best-known lakes, old towns, snow mountains and highland landscapes. High-speed trains, Kunming airport transfers, private car, listed attraction tickets and selected 4–5-star hotels are included.

It is a starting point, not a fixed package. We will tailor the route, pace, hotel choices and visits to your dates, interests, mobility and budget. The sample plan gives Shangri-La a full sightseeing day and uses a direct train back to Kunming, instead of combining sightseeing with a long drive back to Lijiang.

## How the service works

A driver-guide looks after the listed pickups, private vehicle and route coordination. A specialist attraction guide is not included unless it is arranged separately. Vehicles are matched to your party and the route; a specific model cannot be promised. Drivers and vehicles may change between destinations.
