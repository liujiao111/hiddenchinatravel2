## Why this journey

This eight-day private route connects Yunnan’s best-known lakes, old towns, snow mountains and highland landscapes in one itinerary. It is designed for travelers who want those classic destinations without arranging the trains, transfers and hotel pickups themselves.

The pace is full. Days 5 and 6 cover a lot of ground. If you would rather stay two nights in one place, reduce transfers or keep more free time each day, a slower tailor-made version is the better starting point.

## How the service works

A driver-guide looks after the listed pickups, private vehicle and route coordination. A specialist attraction guide is not included unless it is arranged separately. Vehicles are matched to your party and the route; a specific model cannot be promised. Drivers and vehicles may change between destinations.
