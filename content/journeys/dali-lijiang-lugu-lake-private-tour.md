## Why this journey

Western Yunnan changes character as you travel: the open light around Erhai Lake, the stone lanes of Lijiang, the high mountain landscape and the quieter shores of Lugu Lake. This six-day outline gives the lake two nights rather than treating it as a hurried stop.

The source itinerary proposes a full snow mountain day followed by the drive to Lugu Lake. That combination needs a realistic timing check for your travel date; an extra night in Lijiang may be the better choice for a slower trip.

## Is this your kind of journey?

This route suits travelers who want a private vehicle, a mix of well-known scenery and time by the lake, and some flexibility in how each day unfolds. It includes a substantial mountain road transfer and is less suitable if you want every day to be short and unhurried.

## Private travel, local support

Joy helps you shape the route around your arrival, interests and pace. Five nights in the named hotels and a local Chinese- or English-speaking guide are included. The local partner confirms your room arrangement, vehicle and tickets in writing before any booking decision. The written proposal and contract define the services you purchase.
